What it is

A live platform where school students find tutors, book sessions and work towards specific goals, including preparation for competitions.

I built the product, and I tutor physics, computer science, mathematics and biology on it.

Why it exists

The tutoring I could find as a student came in two forms: chain agencies that match on availability and price, and private tutors found by word of mouth, which works if your parents know the right people. Both match on the wrong variable.

What determines whether tutoring works is whether the tutor remembers not understanding the thing.

The person best placed for that is usually two or three years ahead rather than twenty. The platform is built around that. Matching happens on subject and goal rather than on a free slot, and there is a separate view for parents, because the person paying is generally not the person being taught.

What I got wrong

The first version was called RoMaMunich and offered premium one-to-one mentoring. It was too narrow, priced for too few people, and depended entirely on us as individual tutors rather than on the matching being any good. I rebuilt it as a platform rather than a service.

Where it stands

Live, with tutors and students using it.
