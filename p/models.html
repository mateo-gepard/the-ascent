What this is

A set of printable designs I've put out publicly since 2022, starting with a pendulum wall clock when I was thirteen.

They range from furniture-scale objects to a fully printable board game to the housing for an Arduino timer I built at the same time.

Wine bottle stand

The stand supports the bottle at an angle by keeping its centre of mass over a wide, weighted base. The required balance changes as the bottle empties. It has been downloaded around 2,900 times on Printables alone.

Why I keep publishing them

I designed each object because I wanted to use it, then published the files so other people could print it. The designs have been downloaded roughly ten thousand times, which shows that other people found them useful.
