What this is

A set of printable designs I've put out publicly since 2022, starting with a pendulum wall clock when I was thirteen.

They range from furniture-scale objects to a fully printable board game to the housing for an Arduino timer I built at the same time.

The one people actually use

A wine bottle stand that appears to float, holding the bottle at an angle with no visible support. It's a balance problem rather than a clever mechanism: the base has to be heavy and wide enough that the bottle's centre of mass stays over it at any fill level, and a full bottle and an almost empty one are different problems. It has been downloaded around 2,900 times on Printables alone.

Why I keep publishing them

Nobody asked for any of these and nobody was watching. They exist because I wanted the object, and they're public because there was no reason not to make them public.

Roughly ten thousand downloads later, that remains the only thing about them I find genuinely interesting: they're the part of my work where the only reason anyone engaged with it was that it was useful to them.

Where it stands

The designs are all still up and free.
