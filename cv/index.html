Research experience

Apr — May 2024
2 months
Research intern — FRM II research reactor
Technische Universität München · Garching bei München, on site
Mechanical characterisation of an experimental boron-filled 3D-printing filament. Boron absorbs neutrons, so printing with it would let the facility produce neutron-shielding and beam-shaping components in arbitrary geometries rather than machining them — but the printed material's load behaviour was uncharacterised.
  • Printed and prepared tensile specimens across material and print-orientation configurations, three specimens per configuration.
  • Ran tensile tests to failure on a universal testing machine; recorded and compared results against reference materials.
  • Worked to the facility's measurement and documentation standards.
2024 — 2026
BeGreifbar — VR learning research (Jugend forscht, chemistry)
With Juan Rivera Chopinaud and Roman Daugavet
Investigated whether an immersive VR environment improves comprehension of abstract chemistry representations. Built the system (Meta Quest 3, Unity, iPad control surface, web dashboard; ≈31,000 lines of code), then ran an initial study. Results were directional rather than conclusive; a larger sample and interview round are the planned next step.
  • Regional round München-Süd, then the Bavarian state competition (13–15 April 2026), where the project received a special prize.
